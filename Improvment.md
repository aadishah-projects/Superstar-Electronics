
# Main Shortcomings

## 1. Too Much Text on Homepage

The homepage has a lot of descriptive text blocks.

Example:

> “Super Star Electronics now combines a cleaner product-first catalog…”

Most customers will not read this.

### Better:

Keep sections shorter and more visual.

Instead of:

* 3–4 sentence paragraphs

Use:

* icons
* bullet points
* short headings

People buying CCTV usually scan quickly.

---

## 2. Product Cards Need Better Visual Hierarchy

Current product cards are functional but visually flat.

Issues:

* Price does not stand out enough
* “Ask About This Product” looks similar everywhere
* Product images are small
* Brand/model names are too long visually

### Improve:

* Bigger product image
* Bold current price
* Strike-through old price
* Add badges:

  * “Best Seller”
  * “Indoor”
  * “Outdoor”
  * “2K”
  * “WiFi”

This improves click rate significantly.

---

## 3. Homepage Is Too Long

You have:

* Hero
* Products
* Categories
* More products
* Brands
* Services
* Why choose us
* Quote section
* Footer

That becomes exhausting on mobile.

### Better Homepage Structure

1. Hero
2. Top Categories
3. Featured Products
4. Why Choose Us
5. Services
6. Testimonials
7. CTA/Footer

Current homepage feels like:
“homepage + catalog combined together”

---



## 6. Weak SEO Structure

Your SEO is decent but still weak for local search ranking.

You want to rank for:

* CCTV shop in Chitwan
* CCTV installation in Narayangarh
* Hikvision dealer Nepal
* EZVIZ camera Chitwan

Right now, homepage content is generic.

### You should create:

* individual category pages
* blog articles
* location pages

Example:

* `/cctv-installation-chitwan`
* `/wireless-camera-nepal`
* `/hikvision-camera-price-nepal`

This helps Google indexing.

---

## 7. Missing Google Business Integration

I don’t see:

* Google reviews embedded
* Google Maps embed
* ratings

For local businesses this matters hugely.

Add:

* Google review screenshots
* embedded map
* “Directions” button

---



## 10. No Urgency or Offers

Nothing pushes users toward immediate inquiry.

You could add:

* “Free site visit in Chitwan”
* “Installation within 24 hours”
* “1-year support included”
* “Wholesale pricing available”
